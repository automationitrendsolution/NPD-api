Pipeline Overview

Every product idea passes through four evaluation tiers. Each tier gates the next — an idea must pass Tier 1 before Tier 2 runs, and so on. This prevents wasting effort and credits on weak opportunities.

Tier 1
Automated Scoring
Scrape Amazon + BA → 5 sub-scorers → 0–100 score
Tier 2A
Review Scraping
Scrape competitor reviews. Feeds the LLM.
Tier 2C
LLM Analysis
GPT-4o synthesises evidence → VOC + recommendation
Tier 3
Pre-Sourcing
GPT-4o-mini → listing draft + supplier spec
Status Flow
new scored shortlisted pre_sourcing approved
Any stage can lead to: dismissed (low score or human decision)

Tier 1 — Step-by-Step
StepActionWhat happensCost
1Get / Create IdeaLooks up the keyword in MongoDB. Creates a new record if not found. Returns idea_id used in all subsequent steps.Free
2Cooldown CheckIf recently dismissed, scoring is blocked until the cooldown expires. Low-score: 7 days. Hard-kill: 30 days. Use force_rescore=true to bypass.Free
3Already Scored?If tier1_done = true, skips entirely unless force_rescore=true is passed.Free
4Fast Hard-KillChecks keyword against 40+ banned category patterns before any scraping. If matched, idea is dismissed (score 0) + 30-day cooldown. No credits spent.Free
5Fetch SERPScrapes Amazon page 1 via ScrapingBee. Parses 16–24 product cards: ASIN, title, price, rating, reviews, badges, sponsored.1 credit
6BA LookupLooks up the keyword in the local SQLite Brand Analytics database. Returns Search Frequency Rank + top-clicked ASINs with click share.Free
7Select ASINsPicks up to 4 competitors to deep-scrape. Priority: (1) BA top-clicked ASINs, then (2) top organic SERP results. De-duplicated.Free
8Fetch Product PagesScrapes each selected ASIN's full product page. Captures: brand, bullet points, price, rating, review count. Used by Differentiation + Economic scorers.1 × 4 credits
9Run Sub-Scorers5 pure functions run against all collected evidence. Each returns a 0–100 score, component breakdown, and flag list. No API calls.Free
10Compute Composite5 sub-scores combined with fixed weights into one 0–100 composite. If Fit = 0 (hard-kill), composite is forced to 0 regardless of all other scores.Free
11Save to MongoDBFull evidence snapshot (SERP, competitor details, BA row) + scores + flags written to the idea. Status set to scored or dismissed.Free
12Apply CooldownDismissed by hard-kill → 30-day cooldown. Dismissed by low score → 7-day cooldown. Prevents re-scoring junk ideas on every run.Free

Composite Score Formula
Formula
Score =
  (Demand          × 0.35)
+ (Saturation     × 0.25)
+ (Differentiation × 0.20)
+ (Fit            × 0.10)
+ (Economic       × 0.10)
→ clamped 0–100
If Fit = 0 → composite forced to 0. No amount of good demand or economics can save an idea that can't physically be sold.

The 5 Sub-Scores
DemandWeight 35%
How much genuine consumer demand exists for this keyword?
ComponentMax ptsLogic
BA Search Frequency Rank40Amazon's own rank data. Rank ≤500 → 40 pts, ≤2k → 34, ≤5k → 28, ≤10k → 22, ≤25k → 15, ≤50k → 9, ≤100k → 4, else → 2. If no BA data, these 40 pts = 0 and others are rescaled to fill 100.
SERP Organic Depth20Non-sponsored products on page 1. Formula: min(count / 14, 1) × 20. Dense page = keyword is profitable to rank for.
Median Review Count30Median reviews of top 12 SERP products. ≥3000 → 30 pts, ≥1500 → 26, ≥800 → 22, ≥400 → 17, ≥150 → 12, ≥50 → 7, ≥10 → 3.
Sponsored Ratio10Sellers only bid on converting keywords. Formula: min(ratio / 0.30, 1) × 10. 30%+ sponsored = high buyer intent.
🟢 strong_demand (≥75)🔴 weak_demand (<35)⚪ no_ba_data⚪ score_rescaled_no_ba
SaturationWeight 25%
Ease of entry — higher = easier to enter. Inverse of competition strength.
ComponentMax ptsLogic
High-Review Density35Products with ≥500 reviews = strong incumbents. Formula: (1 − ratio) × 35. If 8 of top 10 organic have >500 reviews → ease score is very low.
Badge Concentration25Best Seller + Amazon's Choice badges. Formula: max(0, 1 − badges/5) × 25. 0 badges = 25 pts, 5+ badges = 0 pts.
BA Click Dominance25Top ASIN click share from BA. Formula: max(0, 1 − share/0.50) × 25. >50% share = near-monopoly. No BA data → neutral 12.5 pts.
Price Band Tightness15Wide range = multiple tiers = room to differentiate. Formula: min(range/median, 1) × 15. Narrow band = commoditised.
🟢 low_competition (≥70)🔴 high_saturation (≤30)
DifferentiationWeight 20%
How much room exists to enter with a meaningfully different product?
ComponentMax ptsLogic
Price Gap Opportunity35Wide price spread = multiple value tiers = room for a premium product. Formula: min(gap/1.2, 1) × 35 where gap = (max − min) / median.
Rating Ceiling Gap30If even the best product scores <4.3★, buyers are unsatisfied. Best rating: <3.5 → 30 pts, <4.0 → 25 pts, <4.3 → 20 pts, <4.6 → 12 pts, ≥4.6 → 5 pts.
Brand Diversity20Many brands on page 1 = no loyalty moat. Formula: (unique / total) × 20. One dominant brand = harder to break in.
Feature Whitespace15Vocabulary richness of competitor bullet points. min(unique_keywords / 200, 1) × 15. Clustered features → unmet needs exist.
🟢 strong_differentiation_opportunity (≥70)🔴 commoditised_market (≤25)⚪ low_avg_rating_opportunity
FitWeight 10%
Does this idea fit our operational constraints? Score = 0 forces the composite to 0.
Check / ComponentMax ptsLogic
KILL Keyword patternInstant 0Matches 40+ regex patterns before scraping. Food, books, alcohol, firearms, pharma, digital, live animals → dismissed + 30-day cooldown.
KILL SERP title patternInstant 0≥60% of top-10 titles match non-physical-product patterns (cookbook, paperback, magazine) → killed even if keyword is clean.
KILL Price too lowInstant 0Median organic price <$15. At this level, fees eat all margin. No FBA business is viable below $15.
Price Viability50Price ≥ $15 × 1.3 = 50 pts. $15–$19.50 = 20 pts (marginal). No price data = 25 pts (neutral).
Price Ceiling20Price >$80 = 10 pts (sourcing complexity penalty). Otherwise 20 pts.
FBA Category Signal30Keyword contains FBA-friendly signals (kitchen, home, travel, pet, yoga…). 2+ hits → 30 pts, 1 → 20 pts, 0 → 10 pts.
🔴 hard_kill🔴 price_too_low🟡 price_marginal🟢 strong_category_fit⚪ wrong_product_type
Economic ViabilityWeight 10%
Are the unit economics viable for Amazon FBA? Modelled from median SERP price.
Profit Per Unit (max 60 pts)
Net = Price
 − (Price × 15%)  ← Amazon referral
 − $4.50           ← FBA fee
 − (Price × 33%)  ← COGS target

<$5 → 0 pts    <$7.50 → 20 pts
<$12.50 → 40 pts  <$20 → 55 pts
≥$20 → 60 pts
Monthly Revenue Opportunity (max 40 pts)
Units/mo ≈ Σ(reviews × 0.05)
Revenue = units × median_price

<$3k/mo   → 0 pts
<$9k/mo   → 15 pts
<$24k/mo → 28 pts
≥$24k/mo → 40 pts
🟢 strong_economics (≥70)🔴 poor_economics (≤20)🔴 below_min_profit🟡 marginal_profit🔴 low_revenue_opportunity

Hard-Kill Rules

Instantly dismiss an idea with score 0 + 30-day cooldown. Checked before any scraping to avoid wasting credits.

Keyword Patterns (40+)
Digital/Media book, kindle, dvd, music, software, gift card
Food & Bev food, snack, coffee, tea, supplement, vitamin
Medical prescription, pharmaceutical, FDA approved
Restricted tobacco, vape, alcohol, wine, firearm, gun, ammo
Logistics live animal, live plant, live fish
SERP Title Check

Triggered when ≥60% of top-10 SERP titles match:

cookbook, recipe
paperback, hardcover
magazine, newspaper

Example: "travel journal" passes keyword check but SERP is all notebooks → killed here.

Price Floor ($15)
At $10 selling price:
− Amazon fee: $1.50
− FBA fee:   $4.50
− COGS:     $3.30
= Net: $0.70/unit

No private label FBA business is viable below $15. The price check kills the idea immediately.

Tier 2C — LLM Analysis (GPT-4o)

GPT-4o synthesises all collected evidence into a structured report. It never invents data — it only analyses what Tier 1 and Tier 2A collected.

Inputs to GPT-4o
  • SERP products (titles, prices, ratings, reviews)
  • Competitor detail pages (bullet points, brands)
  • Brand Analytics rank + click share
  • Customer reviews (if Tier 2A ran)
  • Tier 1 scores + score breakdown
Structured Outputs
voc_summary
Voice of customer (3–5 sentences)
pain_points
Specific recurring complaints
dealbreakers
Return reasons / 1-star causes
buyer_avatar
Who buys and who uses it
feature_blueprint
Recommended features vs pain points
recommendation
Go / No-go / Conditional

Tier 3 — Pre-Sourcing (GPT-4o-mini)

Three sequential LLM calls produce actionable documents for manufacturer outreach.

Call 1
Product Concept + Buyer Poll
  • Precise product definition
  • Simulated buyer test vs. top competitor
  • Win/lose verdict with reasoning
Call 2
Amazon Listing Draft
  • Keyword-first title (200 chars)
  • 5 benefit-led bullet points
  • Tagline + image shot list
Call 3
Manufacturer Sourcing Spec
  • Materials, dimensions, packaging
  • MOQ / quote volumes
  • Alibaba search terms
  • Lead-time estimates
